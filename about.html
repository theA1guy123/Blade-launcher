What this is

Blade Launcher is a one-stop launcher for Eaglercraft — Minecraft that runs entirely in your browser. Pick a version, hit launch, and it starts right here. Nothing to install, no account, no Java. Custom clients, resource packs and a handful of launcher settings all live in the same place.

How it works

Each page of the launcher is its own file, loaded only when you open it, so the launcher itself starts fast and you only download the client you actually choose to play. Worlds and in-game settings are saved by each client inside your browser's storage.
