Custom Clients

Modded builds with mod menus, custom GUIs and PvP tools. These replace the vanilla client entirely — pick one and it launches like any other version. Ones marked ONLINE stream from their own site, so they need internet and aren't bundled here.

Custom clients keep their own settings and worlds separate from the normal versions. If a client's mod menu has its own keybind, it's usually Right Shift or \\ — check that client's own menu.
